# Fairway Tales

## [▶ Play Fairway Tales](https://kralnu90.github.io/Fairway-Tales/)

Open the link on your phone, then tap **Install app** in Chrome (or **Share → Add to Home Screen** on iPhone) to keep it on your home screen. It works offline once installed.

A top-down golf RPG. Swipe to swing, shape your shots with spin, collect and level up clubs, and take on the members of Ashby Green Golf Club.

(Formerly Stroke of Luck. Old links forward here automatically.)

## What's in it

- **Story · Chapter 1:** walk around Ashby Green, meet the members, beat Toby, Dot and Marcus, then win the Club Championship.
- **Solo round:** 9 holes, par 36. Earn coins and club XP.
- **Pass & play:** 2 to 4 players on one phone.
- **Practice:** any hole, unlimited undo.
- **Bag & pro shop:** 26 clubs to collect, level and tune up.
- **Leave a review:** tell the developer what you think, right from the menu.

## How to play

- **Swing:** swipe down for your backswing, pause, then swipe up. How straight you swipe shapes the shot.
- **Aim:** drag anywhere on the course to nudge your aim, or tap to jump it.
- **Strike ball:** drag the dot for topspin, backspin, draw or fade.
- **Putt:** pull down on the pad and let go. Chevrons on the green show the slope.
- **Changed your mind?** Slide your thumb into the red zone to cancel a swing.
- **Mulligan:** one per round lets you retake your last shot. You also get one green read (putt preview) per hole.
