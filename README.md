# Fairway Tales

## [▶ Play Fairway Tales](https://kralnu90.github.io/Fairway-Tales/)

Open the link on your phone, then tap **Install app** in Chrome (or **Share → Add to Home Screen** on iPhone) to keep it on your home screen. It works offline once installed.

A top-down golf RPG. Swipe to swing, shape your shots with spin, collect and level up clubs, and take on the members of Ashby Green Golf Club.

(Formerly Stroke of Luck. Old links forward here automatically.)

## What's in it

- **Story:** four chapters. Ashby Green, then Gullhaven Links for the County Qualifier, Sun Mesa for the County Championship, and Frostpeak for the Winter Open.
- **Solo round:** 9 holes on any course you've unlocked: Ashby Green, Gullhaven Links, Sun Mesa or Frostpeak. Earn coins, club XP and reward bags.
- **Challenges:** send friends a link, everyone plays the same pins and wind on their own phone, then compare on a leaderboard.
- **Pass & play:** 2 to 4 players on one phone.
- **Practice ground:** a driving range, a chipping green (fairway, rough or bunker lies) and a putting green with four surfaces.
- **Play a hole:** any hole, unlimited undo.
- **Course creator:** build holes, make courses and share them with codes.
- **Swing difficulty** that follows your handicap, with bigger rewards for better players.
- **Fittings:** heads, shafts and grips in five rarities that trade one stat for another. Trade 3 in for 1 new one.
- **Bag & pro shop:** 40 clubs to collect, level, tune up and forge. Better stock unlocks as your handicap drops.
- **Leave a review:** tell the developer what you think, right from the menu.

## How to play

There's a full guide on the main menu too.

- **Swing:** swipe down for your backswing, pause, then swipe up. How straight you swipe shapes the shot.
- **Aim:** drag anywhere on the course to nudge your aim, or tap to jump it.
- **Strike ball:** drag the dot for topspin, backspin, draw or fade.
- **Putt:** pull down on the pad and let go. Chevrons on the green show the slope.
- **Changed your mind?** Slide your thumb into the red zone to cancel a swing.
- **Mulligan:** one per round lets you retake your last shot. You also get one green read (putt preview) per hole.

## Changelog

### 0.11 · 26 October 2026
- **Challenges.** Play your friends on your own phones. Create a challenge on any course you've unlocked and everyone gets the same pins, the same wind and the same gusts.
- **Send it** as a link (tap to open straight into the challenge) or a code, through WhatsApp or anywhere else. No accounts, no server, works offline.

- Your first round is the one that counts. Replays are just for fun.

### 0.10 · 26 October 2026
- **Swing difficulty.** Hitting it straight is now a skill to earn. The swing reads your whole stroke: drifting sideways on the way down gives you *outside-in* (slice) or *inside-out* (hook), and a bendy upswing curves the ball too. Small swipe errors bend the ball more, timing is stricter and there's more random scatter.
- **Five levels that follow your handicap:** Relaxed (36+, how it used to play), Standard (about 18), Hard (about 5), Very hard (about scratch) and Pro (+5 and better). Your level moves one step at most after each solo round or championship, never mid-round.
- **Harder levels pay:** +10% XP and coins per level above Standard, and better odds of finding fittings.
- **Control and Forgiveness matter more.** They cut down the extra scatter, so fittings like Tacky grips and Cavity heads tighten your shots.
- **Lock your level** in Settings. Pass & play is always Relaxed.
- Story rivals ease off slightly at the harder levels, so matches stay fair.
- Your swing level shows on the main menu, in Settings, on the handicap card in the pro shop and at the end of each round. There's a new How to play section too.

### 0.9 · 26 October 2026
- **Fittings.** Every club now has a head, shaft and grip slot (putters have a head and a grip). There are 14 kinds of part: Max, Lite, Forged and Cavity heads; Stiff, Soft and Tour shafts; Slim (draw bias), Oversize (fade bias) and Tacky grips; plus Blade and Mallet putter heads and Pistol and Fat putter grips.
- **Five rarities,** Common to Legendary. Rarer parts give a bigger boost for a smaller cost.
- **Rare drops:** about 1 in 12 reward bags hold a part. Championship and qualifier bags give better odds.
- **Trade-in:** swap any 3 parts for 1 random part. Its rarity is the average of the three, with a 25% chance of one tier better.
- **Fitting bench:** tap Fittings on any club in your bag to see before and after stats, then fit, move or remove parts.
- **Practice ground,** its own spot on the main menu:
  - **Driving range:** every ball comes back to the mat with carry, total, how far off line and peak height. Calm or breezy.
  - **Chipping green:** partial shots from 15 to 70 yards off fairway, rough or bunker lies. One ball at a time, scored on how close it finishes (average, best, chip-ins).
  - **Putting green:** hole out from random spots on four surfaces: Ashby Green (medium), Gullhaven Links (big and bumpy), Sun Mesa (slow and grainy) and Frostpeak (lightning quick). Pick short, medium, long or mixed distances. Tracks putts per hole and one-putts.
- **Pro shop** sells Common parts for 250 coins.
